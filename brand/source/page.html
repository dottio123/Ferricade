Ferricade motion

Built from the corner F. The rings are true outlines of the F pushed outward, so they start soft and round far away and sharpen into the letter as they pull in.

Loader

3.6 s loop. A ring lands every 1.2 s and the rust tip warms as it arrives.

Intro

Plays once, just under 5 seconds, for first launch. The F gathers, then opens into the wordmark.

    Dropping them into Ferricade

    Each file is one SVG with its own CSS. Inline it, or use it as an <img>. Colours follow the system light or dark setting; on an inline SVG add night or paper to its class to pin a ground.

    Replaying the intro

    CSS animations restart when the node is replaced, so a replay is one line.

    el.replaceWith(el = el.cloneNode(true));

    Reduced motion

    With reduced motion on, the rings are dropped, the loader keeps only a slow warm pulse on the tip, and the intro simply fades in at its final layout.

    Size

    The loader reads best from about 96 px. Smaller than that the rings get hairline thin; the F and its tip still carry it.
